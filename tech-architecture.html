The "Golden Record" Entity Resolution Challenge Core Technical Hurdle
Connecting physical elevator shafts to corporate contract buyers
Why this is the #1 data exercise: At KONE, an elevator has an engineering serial number in the factory, an equipment ID in SAP, an asset record in Salesforce, and a physical location known to municipal cadastral registers. Meanwhile, the entity paying the bill might be an international asset manager (e.g. Blackstone), a national property firm (e.g. Lar España), or a local homeowner association (Comunidad de Propietarios).
STEP 1: Geospatial & Cadastral Normalization

Use GIS reverse-geocoding to resolve disparate address strings ("Calle Gran Vía 28" vs "C. Gran Via, 28, 28013 Madrid") into a canonical Building UUID linked to national land registers.

STEP 2: Probabilistic Graph Linking
