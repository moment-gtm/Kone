Part B | Complete two-act script

The full script follows. [SYNTHETIC] applies to every scenario-specific number, person, operational capability and result, whether or not repeated in an individual line. [KONE] is reserved for reported group facts. Spoken dialogue is fictional and is not a customer or executive quotation.

Brief

A two-act film that shows how the intelligence KONE already holds becomes a growth decision in one market and a launched campaign in another, then returns to the same system as learning. The film opens the CMO workshop, and every screen in it is a discussion prompt rather than a product claim.

Element

Decision

Audience

Petra (CMO) and about 15 extended marketing leaders: regional marketing, CX, customer insights, communications, brand, business marketing.

Objective

Get the room to argue about where KONE already has the signals, where they are fragmented, and what leadership attention is worth giving. Not to sell a platform.

Format

Two acts of two connected sequences, plus an Ingredients transition and closing discussion prompt. Narrated screen film for live playback, readable as a leave-behind.

Tone

Calm, specific, commercially literate. Numbers carry the argument; nothing is described as revolutionary.

Narrative

One Week Modernization proves out in DACH. Iberia, a market with a similar old installed base, is the replication candidate. The loop then closes back into the Control Tower.

Data

All market and campaign figures are defined synthetic data in Part A. KONE business facts are tagged [KONE] and come from the Q3 2026 interim report and presentation.

How to read this script

Each scene has a timecode, a screen and a voice-over. Voice-over is the spoken track. Screen copy is what the viewer reads, and it is written to the same length limits as a real interface so it survives design. Dialogue carries the global-to-local tension that the workshop needs to discuss.

[KONE] marks a fact taken from KONE's Q3 2026 interim report or presentation and can be quoted in the room as stated.

[SYNTHETIC] marks invented scenario data, fully specified in Part A; it is not actual KONE performance.

[APPROVAL] marks a claim or commitment that needs KONE sign-off before it appears on screen.

Titles and arc titles are indicative; KONE will want its own naming conventions.
